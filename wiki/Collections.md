# Collections

A collection is a named set of trophies, for example **Astronaut**: set foot on every Nerospace world. Completing a
collection:

- unlocks a **cabinet trim**, a cosmetic frame for your Trophy Cabinets and Wall Cabinets (see
  [Display Blocks](Display-Blocks));
- shows a "Collection complete" toast;
- for some collections, awards a completion trophy of its own (Boss Hunter awards the nero trophy Four Crowns).

Collections are cosmetic: they unlock nothing in any mod. A collection appears only when every mod its members need is
installed, so a server without NeroCreatures simply has no Boss Hunter. Hidden trophies are never collection members,
and a few collections span several mods for players running the whole family (Supply Chain, Night Out and the Nero
Standard).

Your progress on each collection is in the [Trophy Book](Trophy-Book). Bind a cabinet to a collection to show it off:
the cabinet shows which members are inside and outlines the missing ones.
