# Catalogue: Play and Craft

Trophies for NeroDecor, NeroNotes, NeroArcade, NeroMaps and NeroLink. Each one appears only when its mod is installed.

Tables are generated from the trophy files the mod ships, so they always match the game. Hidden trophies are listed as **???**: they stay hidden until earned, in the Trophy Book and here. "How" says what kind of event earns the trophy; see [Tiers](Tiers) for what the tiers mean.

## Notes

- Catalogue scope: NeroLink has no trophies yet, NeroMaps 4, NeroArcade 5 and NeroNotes 5. More arrive when those mods expose the events.

Trophies are never awarded for work that NeroTrophies cannot see, and they never gate gameplay. Most trophies are also awarded **retroactively**: when you log in, or after a reload, work you already did counts (advancements, statistics, milestones and items you hold). Trophies marked "Not awarded retroactively" are the exceptions.
