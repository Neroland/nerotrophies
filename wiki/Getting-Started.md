# Getting Started

## 1. Install

Put **Neroland Core** (1.23.0 or newer) and **NeroTrophies** in your `mods` folder, plus Fabric API on Fabric. That is
all you need. Every other Nero mod you install adds its own trophies automatically; nothing has to be configured.

## 2. Earn something

Play. Trophies are awarded when you do things: reach a new world, build a machine, tame a creature, defeat a boss,
finish a quest chain. A small toast and a sound tell you when one lands, and its tier sets the sound and the toast
style (see [Tiers](Tiers)).

Work you did before NeroTrophies was installed usually counts too: when you log in, the server quietly checks your
advancements, statistics, milestones and the items you hold, and records the trophies you already deserve. Those
arrive as one summary toast ("N trophies recorded") rather than a burst of noise. A few trophies cannot look back in
time; the catalogue pages mark them "Not awarded retroactively".

On a server with only Core and NeroTrophies you can still earn the vanilla trophies and NeroTrophies' own (Curator
and On Display), see [Core and Vanilla](Catalogue-Core-and-Vanilla).

## 3. Get a Trophy Book

Craft a **Trophy Book** (shapeless): a book, a gold ingot and purple dye. Use it to open your record. You can also
bind a key to "Open Trophy Book" in the controls menu (it is unbound by default). See [Trophy Book](Trophy-Book).

## 4. Claim your trophies

An earned trophy is a record first. To hold one, open the book, pick the trophy and press **Claim**. A genuine trophy
item goes to your inventory (or drops at your feet when it is full). It shows your name on its nameplate and in its
tooltip.

## 5. Show them off

Place the trophy on the ground, stand it on a **Display Pedestal**, hang it on a **Trophy Plaque**, or fill a
**Trophy Cabinet**. Bind a cabinet to a collection and it shows which members are inside and which are still
missing. See [Display Blocks](Display-Blocks) and [Collections](Collections).

## 6. Lost one?

Trophies can be placed, moved and given away; your record cannot. If a trophy is lost or stolen, open the book and
press **Re-claim**. You get a fresh genuine copy and the old one becomes a **replica**: still decorative, but with no
name, and a cabinet never counts it.

## Your name, your choice

Every genuine trophy shows the gamertag of the player who earned it to anyone who can see it. Run
`/nerotrophies showname off` (or untick "Show my name" in the book) to blank your name on every trophy you earned, at
once. See [Privacy](Privacy).
